# Architecture Review Notes (PRD v1.1.1)

Questions and gaps found while turning the PRD into an architecture. Each item needs an
owner and an outcome, usually an ADR or a PRD revision. **Don't resolve legal items here.
Route them to counsel.**

| ID | Severity | Topic | Finding | Proposed direction | Route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | **High** | Tier 2 threshold topology | Plain 2-of-3 lets B+C reconstruct without platform safeguards. | Accepted hybrid policy: B+C is allowed only after the configured delay and with independently verifiable proof that death verification, owner cancellation, dispute/SECURITY_FREEZE handling, and delay requirements were honored. Timer alone is insufficient. The portable mechanism is not yet designed; no B+C sunset implementation before R-25 review. | ADR-003, ADR-012, R-25 |
| R-02 | High | Tier 1 construction | The PRD says card plus designated verifier/auth but does not specify the package key. | Accepted: derive a per-generation key from card secret plus verifier-authorized activation secret using HKDF, then AEAD-wrap K_TIER1; this path cannot reach K_TIER2. | ADR-005 |
| R-03 | High | Share custody for B/C | Trusted-person share custody needs an accessible device and offline recovery path. | Accepted: use passkey PRF where supported; provide QR/printed bearer-secret fallback. Re-seal approval to the request ephemeral key. | ADR-003 |
| R-04 | Medium | State machine | §7 merges the vault heartbeat lifecycle and the unlock request lifecycle into one chain. | Split them into separate machines (STATE-MACHINES.md). | PRD v1.1.2 |
| R-05 | Medium | Terminology | AC-12 still says "MVK" even though v1.1.1 removed the master key. `anonymous_leads` contradicts the rename to "Privacy-Limited Quote". | Change AC-12 to "Tier keys" and rename the table. | PRD v1.1.2 |
| R-06 | Medium | Rollback by omission | CAS stops stale writes, but a malicious server can serve an older consistent snapshot. | Accepted: server CAS plus revision in AAD and per-device last-seen revision. A new device without a trusted checkpoint may not detect an older snapshot. | ADR-013 |
| R-07 | Medium | Metadata leakage | Plaintext tier, object types, sizes and counts reveal vault contents. | Accepted: coarse server-visible types, padded attachment sizes, and item counts for entitlement checks. | ADR-001 |
| R-08 | Medium | Platform escrow protection | The PRD says Share A is encrypted at rest but does not define custody or release authority. | Accepted: KMS/HSM wrapping; release only by the Recovery Orchestrator state-machine identity, with dual control for manual operation. No bypass of safeguards. | ADR-003 |
| R-09 | Medium | Owner loses everything | Losing every device and the recovery kit makes the vault unrecoverable by design. | Accepted: require a printed Owner Recovery Key and onboarding check; routine loss re-wraps keys, suspected compromise rotates and re-encrypts; total device and kit loss is unrecoverable. | ADR-002 |
| R-10 | Medium | Notifications and lawful basis | LINE/email/push escalation sends owner and verifier contact data to messaging processors; SMS and IVR are excluded. | Inventory the actual LINE, email and push providers in the RoPA and processor register. Message templates carry no vault content. | ADR-007, ADR-010 |
| R-11 | Low | Release delay | 48 h is the PRD minimum; owners may want a longer delay. | Accepted: owner-configurable delay with a 48 h minimum, versioned in policy. All recovery paths, including B+C sunset, must honor it. | ADR-003, R-25 |
| R-12 | Low | Coverage-gap assumptions | "Estimated Family Living Cost" needs a versioned assumption source (years × monthly cost, inflation?). | Versioned assumption set in `config/` shipped to the client. | Product |
| R-13 | Legal | Lead model | Whether a fixed CPL counts as insurance solicitation or brokerage is unresolved. | Phase 3 gate. Counsel or OIC must confirm. | Counsel |
| R-14 | Legal | Will templates & Living Will copy | Counsel must sign off on the §1656/§1657 templates and the NHA §12 copy. | Phase 0 gate. | Counsel |
| R-16 | **High / Legal** | New OIC guidelines (B.E. 2569, in force 24 Jul 2026) | These guidelines **post-date the regulatory assumptions in the PRD**. Activities reserved for licensees include links that facilitate contract formation and performance-based compensation tied to policies or premiums. They also require sponsorship and license-status disclosure, and direct inquiries to licensed professionals or the OIC hotline 1186. Whether a per-lead CPL counts as "performance-based", and whether FR-2.2 cards need the "not licensed" disclosure, are open questions. | Get a counsel opinion before Phase 3 design. Meanwhile, keep contextual cards **educational only**, with no purchase or application links. Pay lead fees on a fixed basis that isn't tied to policies or premiums. | Counsel, ADR-009 |
| R-17 | Medium / Legal | Will checklist gaps | The PRD's §1656/§1657 guardrails omit: §1653 (writer/witness and their spouses can't take under the will), §1670 (witness disqualifications), §25 (minimum age 15), §1656 para 2 (alterations must follow the same form), and §1657 (alterations handwritten and signed; no fingerprint in place of a signature). | Add these to FR-1.3 in PRD v1.1.2 and to the signing checklist copy. | PRD v1.1.2, Counsel |
| R-18 | Medium | Benefit model | The SSO funeral grant applies to **non-work-related** death (§33/§39). Work-related death falls under the Workmen's Compensation Fund. §40 benefits depend on the chosen option, and secondary sources disagree on the amounts. | The `config/benefits` schema needs a scheme (SSF §33/§39/§40-option, WCF), an eligibility predicate and a source. Show "check with SSO" where status is unknown. | Product |
| R-19 | Medium / Legal | Electronic wills | I couldn't confirm whether the Royal Decree B.E. 2549 excluding certain transactions from the Electronic Transactions Act covers wills or succession. Secondary sources say e-wills aren't recognized. | Counsel to confirm. Until then, no e-signature or "digital will" claims. Generated files are guides or print packs only. | Counsel |
| R-20 | Medium | The lead domain is not zero-knowledge | Routing a lead requires processing contact details. | Accepted: state this plainly in consent; use a separate lead-domain KMS key and decrypt only after the partner accepts. | ADR-010 |
| R-21 | Medium | Agent sees readiness-complete | Readiness is vault-derived state and commercial partners must not receive it without consent. | Accepted: coarse readiness sharing is opt-in and revocable at any time. | ADR-010 |
| R-22 | Medium | Where HR beneficiary statuses live | FR-3.2 does not define whether statuses are employer or vault data. | Accepted: keep statuses in the vault by default; employee shares a specific status with HR explicitly. | ADR-010 |
| R-23 | Medium | Partner-domain user references | Raw user IDs would allow partner and vault data to be joined. | Accepted: use relationship-scoped pseudonymous references resolved only by identity service. | ADR-010 |
| R-24 | Legal | Lawyer directory and ethics rules | Whether Lawyers Council ethics rules on advertising, solicitation and fee-sharing allow a paid directory with user-requested contact hasn't been checked. | Counsel to confirm before listing law firms. | Counsel |
| R-15 | Low | Ad catalog signing | The signer and key lifecycle need definition. | Accepted: offline signing key and build-pinned public key. Ownership, rotation and revocation remain a Phase 0 gate (R-26). | ADR-016, R-26 |
| R-25 | **High** | Portable Tier 2 sunset authorization | B+C must not bypass death verification, owner cancellation, dispute/SECURITY_FREEZE handling or the configured delay after service exit. | Design and independently review an offline-verifiable protocol proving all safeguards before implementing or shipping any B+C sunset path. No usable Share A may be included in the owner bundle. | ADR-003, ADR-012, Phase 0 gate |
| R-26 | Medium | Catalog signing key lifecycle | Offline catalog signing is accepted, but key ownership, rotation and revocation are not specified. | Define and review the offline signing-key custody, rotation and revocation procedure before production catalog delivery. | ADR-016, Phase 0 gate |
